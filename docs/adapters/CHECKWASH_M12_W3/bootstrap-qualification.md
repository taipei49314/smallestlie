The next source replaces that call with a loop requesting `min(64 KiB, remaining quota + 1)`, continuing after short reads to actual empty-byte EOF. All fresh ancestor/leaf and opened-file checks remain in their original positions around the same handle's reads. No hash/stat cache, projected roster, shortened safety check or timeout increase is introduced.
